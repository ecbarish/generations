Realmbound

A classic-MMO adventure: pick a faction and a class, quest from level 1 to 60 through eight zones, clear four dungeons, found a guild and raid The Hollow Throne. Play it yourself or let Auto take over.

▶ Play Realmbound

Works on computers, tablets and phones. Saves stay in this browser.

Controls

  • Arrows or WASD (or tap) to walk
  • Enter to talk
  • In fights: C combo, D dodge, L loot all
  • Auto plays fights for you when you want a break

Play for the fun. Automate the grind.

Generations is a stable of free adventure and progression games, headlined by Wildbond. Progress is stored in this browser on this device, with automatic backups. Automation is earned in play, never sold.

Play offline or add to your home screen

Open this page online and wait for “Ready for offline play.” You can then open the arcade and its browser games without internet. The new versions of Wildbond and Starfall need internet on their first visit. Use Install arcade when it appears, or your browser’s Install / Add to Home Screen menu.
