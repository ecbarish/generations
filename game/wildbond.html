The headline game · Early preview

Wildbond

Walk into faded Larkhaven, choose a partner and bring colour back with your first bond. Explore all eight regions and take on the league: four courts and the Champion.

▶ Play Wildbond

Best on a computer. The first load needs internet and may take a moment.

Controls

  • Arrows or WASD to walk
  • Enter or E to talk
  • J for the field book
  • M for music
  • On a phone: hold it sideways and use the touch controls

What’s new

  • Oct 14: new ground details around the Larkhaven and Thornwood ponds
  • Oct 14: play on a phone held sideways, by touch
  • Oct 14: phone help on the register, and more room on the partner card
  • Oct 14: a new catalogue batch, with evolved forms

Play for the fun. Automate the grind.

Generations is a stable of free adventure and progression games, headlined by Wildbond. Progress is stored in this browser on this device, with automatic backups. Automation is earned in play, never sold.

Play offline or add to your home screen

Open this page online and wait for “Ready for offline play.” You can then open the arcade and its browser games without internet. The new versions of Wildbond and Starfall need internet on their first visit. Use Install arcade when it appears, or your browser’s Install / Add to Home Screen menu. Your saves stay in this browser on this device.
