Build a frontier guild town. Post jobs for adventurers, serve meals at the inn, work the forge and grow the town with a healer, training yard and apothecary.

Play for the fun. Automate the grind.

Generations is a stable of free adventure and progression games, headlined by Wildbond. Progress is stored in this browser on this device, with automatic backups. Automation is earned in play, never sold.

Play offline or add to your home screen

Open this page online and wait for “Ready for offline play.” You can then open the arcade and its browser games without internet. The new versions of Wildbond and Starfall need internet on their first visit. Use Install arcade when it appears, or your browser’s Install / Add to Home Screen menu. Your saves stay in this browser on this device.
