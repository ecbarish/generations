## Otherworld (working title) — anime isekai

Summoned or reincarnated into another world. You get stronger, unlock powers and progress through story arcs.

- **Status window** as the main screen: level, stats, titles, skills, the classic isekai readout.
- **Skills that evolve:** using a skill levels it; at milestones it evolves into a stronger form or merges
  with another into a Unique Skill.
- **Story arcs** as the progression spine: each arc is a chapter with its own region, cast, villain and a
  boss you have to be strong enough to beat.
- **Adventurer guild ranks** F → S, gated by quests and arc bosses.
- **Companions** who join through the story, each with their own growth.
- **Reincarnation** as the reset: die, or choose to start over, and carry chosen skills into the next life.
  Fits the genre exactly, and opens loop-style story beats where knowledge from past lives matters.
- **Automation as skills:** *Auto-Battle*, *Parallel Thinking*, summoned familiars that farm for you.

**Roguelike runs: each life is a run.**
- **The goddess's gift:** every new life opens in the white room with a draft of random cheat skills.
  Pick one or two; they shape the whole run.
- **Random rebirth:** born as a noble's third son, a village orphan, a slime, a spider, a sword. Each start
  changes stats, which skills you can learn and which story arcs open.
- **Random events** along the way: a dungeon break, a demon-lord sighting, a rival summoned hero.
- **Soul points** carry over: spend them on permanent upgrades, keeping skills across lives, and unlocking
  new rebirth types and goddess gifts. Loop-style story beats reward what you learned last life.

Overlap to watch: Starfall Guild is about running a guild; Otherworld is one protagonist's story.

## Creatures, companions and taming (Evan, 2026-10-06)

Roots: WoW hunters and Pokemon. Kairosoft's horse racing game nailed the raising fantasy; Ashes of Creation
promised it and never shipped. Depth here is rarely explored.

- **Taming and raising:** catch creatures in the wild, then feed, train and bond with them. Use them in battle,
  as mounts for travel, and in races.
- **Rarity from common to mythical:** generic species plus named unique creatures. Rarer ones are harder to
  find, tame and raise.
- **Hybrids:** rare wild crossbreeds, and breeding for new combinations with inherited traits.
- **Who you are matters:** classes and races interact with creatures differently (a hunter tames what a mage
  can only bind; a druid race bonds faster with forest beasts).
- **Companions:** party members with relationships that grow; learning to work together unlocks combined
  abilities.

Where it lives: a standalone creature game for the full depth, plus hunter pets, mounts and companions inside
Realmbound. A shared creature system could feed both.
