Phone (390×844) and desktop (1280×800) shots of each change, made by `shots.cjs` (needs Playwright and Chrome; run it from this folder with `NODE_PATH` pointing at Playwright).

- `*-1-ranch-sign-wiggle`: the ranch left alone, a sign mid-wiggle (LRL-10).
- `*-2` to `*-6`: the Dough Table. The four colour dots, the dough turning pink then blue when a dot is tapped, a blue cookie stamped, and the cookie hopping clear of the cutter (LRL-6; LRL-8/9 stay fixed from #424).
- `*-7`, `*-8`: feeding the fox. Hearts, a hop and a bowl bounce at the moment of the tap, then eating (LRL-7).
- `*-9` to `*-11`: the Paint Shed left early. Two parts painted, then the barn picture: she trots home from the sign, the colours puff out, then she waves (LRL-15).
- `*-12`, `*-13`: the bedtime cue (toothbrush), bigger, with a soft glow, still waiting 17 seconds in (LRL-13). `before-phone-12-bedtime-cue.png` is the old size on main.
- `*-14`: the dusk fade at 8 seconds, about halfway (LRL-14).
