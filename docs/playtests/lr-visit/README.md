# LR-VISIT screenshots

These five pictures are the Meadow a moment after a visit starts. The grown-up panel is closed. Sound is on, which is the default. Nothing from the visit is stored.

The sizes are the five Little Ranch review sizes. 820 by 1180 and 390 by 844 are captured at a device pixel ratio of 2. The others are captured at 1.

The sound choice is not a picture. The Little Ranch page reloads the real document twice for Sound off and twice for Sound on, and the saved choice comes back each time. That check is inside tests/little-ranch.html. `node tools/run-all-checks.cjs` runs it.
