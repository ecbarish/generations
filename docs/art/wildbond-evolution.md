# Wildbond evolution: humble beginnings, spectacular payoff

**Evan's call, 2026-10-13.** This is the rule for every evolution line in Wildbond: its art, its names, its dex lines and
the moment it evolves. Written up by Claude the same day; the catalogue below applies it to every existing line.

## The rule

**Humble beginnings, spectacular payoff.**

1. **Base forms stay safe and normal.** Real animals, grounded and charming. A creature you'd believe exists.
2. **Evolutions transform; they don't just grow.** The final form feels like an event (the Tyranitar or Salamence kind
   of payoff), not a bigger copy.
3. **Every base form plants a visible promise, and the final form cashes it in.** The fantasy is there from the start
   (a Shelgon dreaming of flight, a Pupitar building mass); evolution fulfils it. These examples show the pattern;
   never copy them.
4. **The middle stage may be the awkward cocoon beat**, the ugly-duckling phase. It makes the payoff hit harder.
5. **It stays the same creature throughout.** This is the bond: your partner ages with you; it is never replaced.
   Change the silhouette a lot, make new abilities visible (wings, an elemental aura, a new posture) and raise its
   presence, but never redesign it into a different animal.

### The test for every line

- **Cover the middle stage.** Look at the base and the final form: can you see the promise that connects them?
- **Cover the base.** Look at the middle and the final form: does the final still feel like a reward?

A line that fails either test is redrawn before it ships.

### What this means for each kind of work

- **Art (lane G and any artist):** draw the promise into the base sprite (a folded sail, banked embers, a seed in the
  beak), so a player can point at it. The final form's silhouette must be readable as a different shape at a glance,
  with the promise now fulfilled and visible.
- **Names and dex lines (lane A):** the base form's dex line hints at the promise ("it dreams of...", "it practises...").
  The final form's dex line says it came true, in the world's words.
- **The evolution moment (lane W):** the scene sells the payoff: a pause, the old silhouette, the light, the new
  silhouette, and the partner showing off its new ability once. It must never feel like swapping one creature for another.

## Single-form creatures

About sixty creatures have one form. They fall into three groups:

- **Guardians** (Elderhorn, Breakwatermane, Hearthcrown, Lanterncrest, Stillwake, Undertone, Meadowmantle, Watchlight,
  Veilmote): already the spectacle. They don't evolve.
- **"It never evolves" on purpose** (Hearthlaugh, Cinderstitch, Laughrill, Fordfoal): their dex lines make staying
  the same the point. Keep them.
- **Candidates for future lines**, whose dex lines already plant a promise. When the catalogue grows (Phase 2-4 of
  docs/STUDIO-PLAN.md), turn the best into lines first:
  - **Drakelet**: "it dreams of becoming a dragon". The textbook promise.
  - **Galefoal**: "born to race".
  - **Emberling**: it glows faintly at night.
  - **Glimmerwing**: feathers that catch the light like glass.
  - **Fogtail**: it vanishes in cloud except for its tail.
  - **Kiteskirl**: rides the breeze without flapping.
  - **Pebblepaw**: sharpens its claws on granite.
