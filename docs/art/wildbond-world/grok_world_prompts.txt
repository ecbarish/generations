WILDBOND WORLD CONCEPT ART: PROMPT SHEET FOR GROK BOT'S IMAGE GENERATOR
=======================================================================
Sources: the world design doc (PR #455 at c47af4fa, sections 2 and 5), the world references (#452, section 5), the art direction
page (docs/art/wildbond-art-direction.md section 3 ramps) and the house rules (docs/art/README.md). Names come only from those.
Output: 16:9, 1280x720 PNG, saved as /workspace/wildbond/world-map/gen/<slug>.png (slug given on each prompt).
These are concept and mood images for planning. They are reference only, not shipped art, and they never replace the code
painter (#455 hard rule 11).

HOUSE STYLE (applies to every prompt; paste it under each one)
- "A storybook valley that lost its colour and is getting it back." Old-souled, handmade, never candy-bright. The greens are deep and cool, stone and timber are warm, roofs are slate blue, and barns are red.
- Light comes from the upper left, and the shadow side is lower right.
- Every lit window and lamp is warm lamplight #f2d080. Outlines and the darkest accents are warm near-black ink #1e1a22, never pure black.
- Real proportions, no chibi. A person is about 1.5 tiles tall, a cottage is a whitewashed 4-by-4-tile house with a steep slate roof, and Maren's barn is red with a gambrel roof.
- Simple readable shapes first (boxes, roof prisms, cylinders, cones), with detail inside the shapes.
- NO text, letters, numbers, signs with writing, map labels, borders, compass roses or UI. Labels are stamped on later by script.
- No creatures in the foreground unless a prompt asks for one. No people bigger than a few pixels in the map prompt. No logos, no franchise look, no copied maps.

0. ILLUSTRATED OVERWORLD MAP  slug: overworld-map
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
A painted fantasy region map of one valley, seen from above at a top-down three-quarter angle, like a hand-painted storybook
endpaper. 16:9. Light from the upper left. Muted storybook colour, a little washed out at the edges as if the colour is still
returning. NO text or labels of any kind.
Layout (north is up; keep these positions, because labels are overlaid later at fixed points):
- A grey-blue sea runs along the whole top edge from the far left to about 60% across (Water #2f5f7e #3a7090 #4a84a2, glints #8cc0d4). Near the top middle of that sea sits one low, pale sand island with a single small shelter (Outer Shoal). Near the top left, a small dark sea-cave arch sits at the waterline (Keelcave).
- Top left, under the sea: a sandy salt-marsh coast with a tide line of weed and shells, a small quay with a low inn, net reels and a lighthouse behind it (Saltmarsh Coast and Lantern Quay). A round pool sits a little inland on the west side (Moon Pool), with a channel mouth toward the sea. Sand #d2b886 #e0c896 #ead6a8.
- Left middle: deep cool woodland of broadleaf and pine, with a settlement at a hawthorn gate, a stream, a hidden grove to its east and a root-hollow cave (Thornwood, Old Root Grove, Knothollow). Leaf #24503e #2f6a4a #3f8558 #5ea26a, Pine #1f4a44 #2a5e54 #37766a.
- Bottom left corner: a small village of whitewashed cottages with slate roofs and one red barn, lanes and meadow, with a black burrow arch in a grass bank to its west (Larkhaven, Millgrass Lane, Larkburrow).
- Centre left: warm ash-brown highlands climbing in stair-cut steps, with hot springs and a spring-house venting steam, and a red-glowing cave mouth in a black hill (Emberfall Highlands: Ashstair, Warmstep, Kilnvent). Barn red #6e2620 #8e342c only as ember glow.
- Top centre, just under the sea: a mountain pass of slate-blue rock with a roped lodge, a sheer drop (a cliff lip with a view down to the basin), and a path climbing to an open bare summit (Cloudglass Pass: Highlatch, Glassfall, Crest Path, Lampcrown). Slate #33465e #435a78 #56708f #7290ad.
- The centre: the hub, a broad basin of reed channels and boardwalks on still water, with a low boathouse, a dry shed, a raised orchard to the east, and a stair that goes down into dark water (Stillreed Basin: Reedhold, the Long Boards, Orchard Fen, the Quiet Channels, Lowbell).
- Bottom centre: rolling grey-green hills with worn flagstones, a small hamlet with a bell house, a quarry cut and a cave mouth in the rock (Hollowecho Hills: Bellstep, Flintcut, the Answering Ways). Flagstone greys #6e6e62 #8c8c80 #a4a496.
- Centre right: bright open commons, meadow and posts with ribbons, a long meeting hall, and a grey mill beside a meadow, its wheel still (Sunthread Commons: Threadmeet, Ribbon Road, Greymill). Grass #4f8a50 #5c9a5a #6aa862 #7cb86c.
- Top right: high sea cliffs and a stone lookout with a ledger house, a path down to a pier on the sea (Farwatch Reach: the High Watch, Seaward Ledge).
- Far top right corner: a stair climbing to a court on a height, and a slim tall spire to its west (the Returning Light League and the Lighthouse Spire).
- Thin pale paths join everything, and the main road snakes from the bottom-left village up the west side, east across the highlands and pass, through the central basin, then east and north to the cliffs and the far top-right court.
Keep generous empty ground around each landmark (labels go there later). Lamplight #f2d080 in a few windows only.

1. THORNWOOD  slug: region-thornwood
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Mood key art, 16:9, a top-down three-quarter view of a pixel-art-inspired storybook scene, painted. Morning, low sun from the upper left
through deep cool trees. Thornwood: a settlement at a hawthorn gate path with a small loft above the gate (the Gate Loft),
whitewashed cottages with slate roofs. To one side, a lane under broadleaf crowns and stacked pines (Thornwood Trail), a short
meadow cut (Dewcut), and a stream that floods where mushrooms grow (Rootwater Path; the stream is still water, not a road).
In the grove to the east, a ring of old trees with a living curtain of plants (it must read as plants, not a fence) and a carved
antler sign with no writing (Old Root Grove). A low cave under a root ceiling with one pale crack (Knothollow).
Palette: Leaf #24503e #2f6a4a #3f8558 #5ea26a #8ccf7e; Pine #1f4a44 #2a5e54 #37766a #4f9480; Bark #4a3426 #6a4a32 #8a6444;

2. SALTMARSH COAST  slug: region-saltmarsh
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Mood key art, 16:9, top-down three-quarter. Late afternoon, sun from the upper left over a grey sea. Saltmarsh Coast's dunes
and marsh, a tide line of weed and shells (Wrackline) leading to Lantern Quay: net reels, a net loft, and a low quay inn that is a
whitewashed cottage with a blue door, with a lighthouse behind it. An early pier. Inland, a round still pool (Moon Pool) whose far
lip opens into a channel to the sea. Show the two kinds of water clearly apart: the pool is still, and the channel and the sound
(Greywash Sound) carry a visible current pattern and a small wake. At the waterline, a keel-scraped sea-cave arch (Keelcave).
The sea is grey until the colour is back, but honest.
Palette: Sand #d2b886 #e0c896 #ead6a8; Water #2f5f7e #3a7090 #4a84a2 #8cc0d4; Whitewash #cfc4ac #e6dcc6 #f4eddc;
Slate #33465e #435a78 #56708f #7290ad; Grass #4f8a50 #5c9a5a; lamplight #f2d080 in the inn window and the lighthouse; ink #1e1a22.

6. HOLLOWECHO HILLS  slug: region-hollowecho
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Mood key art, 16:9, top-down three-quarter. Night, a cool moon from the upper left, warm lamplight in the hamlet. Rolling grey-green
hills with worn flagstone paths (The Late Path), a quarry cut through loose stone (Flintcut), and Bellstep: a hamlet with a bell
house and a bell hanging beside it. In the rock face, a real cave mouth whose first hall glows softly from small radiant motes
and the bell outside (the Answering Ways). Deeper inside it is dark, but the floor near the mouth is still readable. A second, older
painted-dark mouth sits nearby as scenery. Quiet, a sense of answers arriving a beat late.
Palette: flagstone greys #6e6e62 #8c8c80 #a4a496 (Hollowecho's painted floor in main.gd); Slate #33465e #435a78 #56708f;
Grass #4f8a50 #5c9a5a; Whitewash #cfc4ac #e6dcc6; lamplight #f2d080 for the bell house and the motes; ink #1e1a22.
Darkness reads as a soft overlay, never as black paint: keep cave floors above the ink tone.
