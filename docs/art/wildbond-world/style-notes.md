# Wildbond world: style notes per region, from 2D now to modern 3D later

This is a planning note to go with the world schematic and the Grok prompt sheet. Places and links come from the world design doc (PR #455 at 66647dc9, its final approved head) and the world references (#452). Colours come from the ramps in `docs/art/wildbond-art-direction.md` section 3, plus Hollowecho's and Sunthread's flagstone colours, which are painted in `wildbond-godot/scripts/main.gd` around line 2245 but are not on the ramp table yet. Nothing here is canon until the matching phase is accepted.

## Rules that hold in every region and every tier

- **Scale.** The locked v3 overworld scale (Evan, 2026-10-13) sets the door line at 24px: a 2.0 m door at 12.35 px per metre, which is 1.5 tiles. The tamer is about 12×21. Walking pixels in the pixel tiers follow the locked curve: px = round(metres × 12.35); at 24 or under the height is px with a floor of 12, and above 24 it is 24 + 8 × log2(px / 24), rounded and capped at 32. So the four 2.0 m Legends walk at 24px (not 25) and Meadowmantle at 2.66 m walks at 28px (not 32). `figures.gd` `drawn_px` still uses a plain 12–32 clamp, which #455 marks as code to be fixed, not the rule. HD-2D (T3) does not use the curve: it draws about metres × 12.35, clamped to 10–48px. The 3D tier uses true metres and the 2.0 m door. A cottage is 64×64 with a 12×24 door, and Maren's barn is 64×80 with 16×26 doors. #455 writes this into the art direction page ("Scale (locked v3)"): a 16px tile is about 1.30 m, and the painter's cottage and barn cells catch up in a later paint pass.
- **Light.** The light comes from the upper left, and every material has a light, a middle and a dark value, with the dark side lower right. Lamplight is always `#f2d080`, and the ink is always `#1e1a22`. Lanterns, embers, Bellmote-light and the bell are additive glows laid over sprites that keep their upper-left shading. In HD-2D they become real point lights, and the sun stays upper left.
- **Darkness is an overlay.** Dark caves and night are a game overlay on top of the sprites, never painted into the tiles (`docs/art/README.md` rule 1). The darkest walkable floor stays clear of the ink. The first Cave proposal (`#1a1c22`, `#2a2e38`, `#3e4656`) reached only 1.01, 1.26 and 1.81 to 1 against the ink, so #455 replaces it with a cool stone that clears 4.5:1: Cave dark `#82848e` (4.60:1, the floor and the wall, and as dark as any tile a tamer stands on), Cave mid `#969ea4` (6.30:1) and Cave light `#b0b0bf` (8.00:1, the top-left face), on a cave palette of its own. The only darker shade is `#2a2e38`, used inside a mouth past the first walkable tile and never as a floor. Counted with the ink, Lamplight and the ramps a cave borrows, that palette is 24 colours, or 25 with the mouth shade, still under the cap. Proposal for the director: before Lantern, a ring of floor about three tiles around the tamer stays readable, and the way out and any cracks stay visible.
- **Rideable water reads by pattern, not only colour.** The Water ramp's two middle shades are only 1.31:1 apart (`#3a7090` against `#4a84a2`), so a colour-only difference disappears in the 4-shade Pocket tier. Rideable tiles carry a current line or chevron pattern and a wake. Solid `~` ponds stay still, with a glint and a lily pad and no wake (#455 hard rule 3). Rideable water needs four shore edges, four outside corners and four inside corners for bays and channel bends. At Moon Pool's lip, one tile of `#8cc0d4` sits between solid and rideable water so the meeting still reads when colours collapse.
- **Big creatures.** Anything taller than the 24px door line waits outside. The huge wolf, horse, boar, croc and hyena ride as mounts, while a creature of 24px or less follows indoors, including Tidewyrm at 15px. A taller creature may crawl in only when its crawl pose is 12px tall or less and at least twice as long as it is tall, which today means Deeptide in that pose only. Every healer door gets a clear 2-by-2-tile waiting pad beside it. A mount may walk into a cave only where the mouth and ceiling are both at least 48px (3 tiles), per #455's cave table. Knothollow's ceiling is 32px, so no mount goes in, and Elderthorn and Elderhorn both wait at its mouth.
- **Palette budget.** Each palette stays at or under about 32 colours (`docs/art/README.md` rule 6). The world painter (`wildbond-godot/tools/paint_tiles.gd`) already uses 54 distinct colours, so caves and regions should use palette swaps instead of adding shades to the world palette. The per-region counts below are for the prompt-sheet lists, ink and lamplight included.
- **What carries from 2D into 3D.** These must survive every tier: the silhouettes, the footprints matching the pictures (`buildings()`), the ramp colours as one material each, the upper-left sun and the ink outline as a toon line.

## Thornwood (Thorn Badge, Isolde)
**Now (T1 Pocket and T2 Pixel):** Leaf, Pine, Bark, Grass, Path, Water (the stream stays solid `~`) and Whitewash with Slate roofs. The prompt list is 31 colours, right at the cap, so the 3D tiers can keep the fifth Leaf shade `#8ccf7e` and the fourth Pine shade but the pixel palette drops them. Key tiles are the broadleaf and pine treeline, tall grass, path with pressed stones, and the stream. The landmarks are the hawthorn gate with the Gate Loft above it, Old Root Grove's ring (Elderhorn's site is its oldest trees, on the side a Stone partner can already walk) with a living curtain that reads as plants (not a fence), the antler sign, and Knothollow's root ceiling with a pale crack. In Pocket, the curtain has to read by shape, as a soft leaf edge against a hard trunk line, not by its green.
**Later (HD-2D and 3D):** bark and moss materials, crowns modelled as a big sphere plus five smaller clusters, and morning shafts of light from the upper left. Knothollow stays short (48px wide, 32px tall), a supply cave that no mount enters, not a second Thornwood.

## Saltmarsh Coast (Tide Badge, Nerys)
**Now:** Sand, Water, Whitewash, Slate and Grass, 18 colours. Key tiles are dune sand, marsh grass, the Wrackline tide line of weed and shells, the pier, still pool water and the new rideable water. The landmarks are Lantern Quay's low inn, a cottage with a blue door under the lighthouse, the net reels, Moon Pool's lip where still water meets rideable water, and Keelcave's keel-scraped arch at the waterline. Breakwatermane's site is the Wrackline beach where the tide draws back, outdoors, so the beach needs open sand for a 28px mount. The lip is the one place both kinds of water touch, so the pattern change has to be crisp there.
**Later:** wet sand that reflects, a sea material with flow and depth colour, and the lighthouse as a cylinder massing that reads from Wrackline. Keelcave stays dry inside, with one ledge.

## Emberfall Highlands (Ember Badge, Toren)
**Now:** Path, Barn red (used only as ember), Whitewash, Slate for the dark hill, and Bark, 17 colours. #455's "warm stone" is not on any ramp yet, so it needs a ramp before use. Key tiles are the stair-cut ash steps of Ashstair, the springs and the Sootpath track. The landmarks are Hest's spring-house with its round vent, lit window and steam (an animation over the house that never blocks the door), and Kilnvent's red mouth in a black hill, readable from the coast climb. Hearthcrown's site is the Warmstep spring terrace, outdoors, so the terrace needs room for a 31px mount. The first room of Kilnvent is readable without Lantern. The deep room is dark at the edges and warm at the centre through an overlay glow, not red tiles.
**Later:** emissive ember material, steam particles that hold one frame under reduced motion, and a vent point light in HD-2D. Kilnvent's mouth should be wide enough for the region's large Ember creatures to be seen at it.

## Cloudglass Pass (Beacon Badge, Vessa)
**Now:** Slate, Pine, Whitewash and Path, 16 colours. Key tiles are slate rock, scree, pines and the rope posts. The landmarks are Highlatch's lodge (Vessa's shelter, given a door), Ilka's ropes, Glassfall's cliff lip with a view down, Pass Foot winding down, and Lampcrown's open summit. Lanterncrest (Radiant, 24px) sits exactly on the door line, and Lampcrown is a clear 4-by-4-tile pad so the bird and the tamer stand apart. The last stair of Lampcrown needs Daymark.
**Later:** cold rim light, mist layers below the lip, and the summit as an open plateau mesh. Glassfall's drop is the cue for its one-way rule: no stair back up.

## Stillreed Basin (Reed Badge, Olan), the hub
**Now:** Water, Bark (all the boards), Grass, Whitewash, Slate and Leaf, 20 colours. Key tiles are the boards, reed water with rideable channels, the orchard and the jetty. The landmarks are Juna's low boathouse inn (not a second lighthouse), Colm's dry shed with a slate roof, the ferry house, the moored skiff (scenery, solid), the Dry Boards as a covered lamplit walk that is clearly not a cave, and Lowbell's stair into dark water, visible from the jetty before it can be ridden. Stillwake's site is the Reedhold jetty, the surface crossing, with its 2-by-2 pad. Lowbell is a drowned landing with a bell, and lamplight falls on the bell only.
**Later:** wood planks and wet wood, reflections of sky and lamps on the still water, and the Quiet Channels with a flow direction. Lowbell is lit only by its bell glow, with no shrine architecture.

## Hollowecho Hills (Echo Badge, Senna)
**Now:** the cool flagstone greys from `main.gd` (`#6e6e62`, `#8c8c80`, `#a4a496`), Slate, Grass, Whitewash and lamplight, 12 colours. Those greys should go onto the ramp table. Key tiles are worn flagstones, rock and the quarry cut of Flintcut. The landmarks are Bellstep's bell house (given a door on its existing footprint) with the bell beside it, and the Answering Ways' real mouth. It must not be confused with the reveal mouth at tile (19, 8), which stays painted scenery (#455 hard rule 4). The director needs three states: the lit first hall, Lantern dark, and the Hush door that looks like wall. Undertone's site is the first bend of the Answering Ways, so the mouth is 48×48px. Veilmote's site is an unowned nest ledge on The Late Path's night path, after Hush.
**Later:** damp stone with a soft specular sheen, Bellmote motes as small point lights, and a night grade that keeps the floor readable. Echoes are a sound cue, not a visual.

## Sunthread Commons (Loom Badge, Halen)
**Now:** Grass, Whitewash, Barn red (small ties only), Path, the warm flagstones from `main.gd` (`#9a8a64`, `#c4b48c`), Bark and Slate, 17 colours. Key tiles are open meadow, ribbon posts, shelter ties and warm flagstones. The landmarks are Threadmeet's meeting hall (Solla's healer room on the hall that is already drawn), Pell's counter as a stall under the eaves, Greymill (whitewash gone grey, a wheel that does not turn until the errand's last knot, and a stuck door), and the nursery gate. Meadowmantle's site is the Threadmeet nursery, outdoors, and as a 28px mount it needs open space there. The Larkburrow mouth at the back of Threadmeet should be recognisable as the back of the hall hill.
**Later:** cloth ribbons with gentle wind motion, a mill wheel mesh that holds still, and bright, even afternoon light.

## Farwatch Reach (Horizon Badge, Rysa)
**Now:** Slate, Sand, Whitewash, Water and Grass, 18 colours. Key tiles are cliff, ledge path, pier, open rideable sea and the sand island. The landmarks are the High Watch's lookout, Quen's ledger house (a stone room with a desk, not a cottage), the Chart Road loop, Seaward Ledge down to the pier (where fishing happens) with the harbor light, which is Watchlight's site, and Outer Shoal, a low sand island with one shelter and no town.
**Later:** wind, spray and fog layers, a sea with large-scale flow, and cliff massing that shows the drop. The harbour lantern is the one strong point light.

## Outside the eight regions
Larkhaven (home) keeps its cottages, Maren's barn and the Larkburrow mouth: a 48×48px arch in a grass bank on Millgrass Lane, outlined in the ink `#1e1a22` with the mouth shade `#2a2e38` inside past the first walkable tile, because Cave dark alone sits too close to the grass to read as an opening. The League and the Lighthouse Spire keep their courts and Orla's floors. Trialstair, the cave before the league, repeats each lesson's obstacle in small (crack, dark, flood, thicket, false wall, faded stone), so it inherits each region's look in turn.
