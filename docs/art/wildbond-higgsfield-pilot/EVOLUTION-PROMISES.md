# Humble beginnings, spectacular payoff

**Evan's decision, 2026-10-13.** This supersedes the pilot's earlier allowance for final forms that only mature naturally. Every evolving family begins as a grounded, charming animal and ends with a substantial transformation. A small visible promise connects those endpoints. The same bonded individual grows through the line; evolution never substitutes an unrelated animal.

The transformation may change posture, silhouette, anatomy and elemental expression. It must preserve recognisable identity anchors, and visible capabilities should follow the creature's own story. A middle stage can be awkward, restrained or transitional; the final stage must feel rewarding without relying on the middle stage to explain its connection to the base.

## Scope and source of truth

Checked the Godot catalogue: `wildbond-godot/data/wildbond.json` plus `wildbond-godot/data/evolution.json`, using the same override rules as `tools/wildbond-catalog.cjs`. There are **15 live evolving families and 17 live terminal outcomes**; Poolkit has three outcomes. Browser-only evolution links miss the starters' final stages. Single-stage species have no evolution payoff to assign; this document does not invent evolutions for them.

The table gives **proposed art briefs under Evan's approved rule**. The relationships and existing behaviour come from game data; new visual treatments require reference approval and do not implement moves or abilities. None of the game data, art renderers, evolution requirements or saves change here.

## Outlined branches that are not live Godot links

`docs/lore/catalogue-evolution.json` outlines two extra Saillet outcomes, **Fogsail** and **Sunfrill**, alongside Draftscale. They are present as species in the catalogue but the inspected Godot evolution links still take Saillet to Draftscale only. Preserve this distinction; do not call either branch playable based on this art brief.

- **Fogsail:** the same small ribbed-sail promise becomes a broad soft-edged mist canopy around a recognisable lizard; the payoff is shelter and concealment, with rib structure readable through the haze.
- **Sunfrill:** the same promise becomes a radiant, fully opened sun-catching frill with large warm translucent panels; the payoff is an unmistakable sunburst silhouette while retaining the same head, legs and sail attachment.

## What changes in the current pilot

- **Cindercub, Ripplet and Mosshog:** keep grounded base anatomy; make the promise visible with one restrained feature, not miniature final-stage spectacle. Correct Cindercub's pointed generated ears toward its round-ear brief.
- **Blazefang and Thornback:** these are middle-stage studies. Their restrained maturation can be useful here, but neither is the final reward.
- **Current Sail Tidewyrm:** also a middle-stage study. Its crest must leave room for Deeptide's much stronger flowing silhouette. Do not finalise Tidewyrm's scale or ornament before sketching Deeptide beside it.
- **Pyremane test:** compare Cindercub directly with two final-stage directions, Hearth Crown and Storm Standard. Judge the promised ruff-to-crown connection with Blazefang hidden.

## Reference acceptance and generation brief

Every family packet now requires: a grounded base; one visible promise sentence; a final payoff sentence; two persistent identity landmarks; endpoint silhouettes shown at equal height; the middle hidden for the reward test; and a separate check that all stages are the same individual. An awkward middle is allowed; an arbitrary animal swap is not.

Use this addition in future generation prompts:

```text
Rule: HUMBLE BEGINNINGS, SPECTACULAR PAYOFF.
Base animal and individual identity: [canon].
Visible promise in base: [one restrained feature].
Actual final species, including Godot overrides: [ID].
Final payoff: [new silhouette and visible capability].
Retain these identity landmarks: [A] and [B].
Middle stage: [what develops; what remains reserved for the final].
Compare the base and final at equal height with the middle hidden.
Do not achieve the payoff merely by enlargement or by swapping animals.
```
