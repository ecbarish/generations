# Tagline change (Flint, 2026-10-12)

**Decided (Evan, 2026-10-12): "Play for the fun. Automate the grind."** Gemini suggested it; Evan picked it over the
options below. It fits GOALS.md's "earned automation, not autoplay": the fun is played, the chores you have mastered
can be automated. The "no automation words" rejection below is overruled by Evan's pick. The GitHub description edit
is Evan's own step on github.com (docs/proposals/rename-checklist.md, "Proposed order").

Evan, 2026-10-12: the tagline needs work or removal — "I don't want to be corny." Also, honest
monetization (ads, microtransactions) is on the table if the games can genuinely earn, so the
branding must not promise "no ads ever." The project started as a hobby and has become more;
making money from it would be awesome, but it was never the plan.

## The short version (revised 2026-10-12)

Evan: no guarantee they stay browser games (Godot for PC, Little Ranch as an app). The brand
is fun games born from real people, real experiences and preferences — not big studios.
"Browser" is out.

**Recommended: "Fun games from real life. Play, don't watch."**

Why it wins:
- Says what they are (fun games) and where they come from (real life) in six words.
- Keeps the 2026-10-12 "Play, not watch" pivot as the second half.
- Makes no promise about ads or monetization — nothing to walk back later.
- Plain description, not a slogan trying to be clever. Not corny.

Alternatives, best first:
1. **"Real games, made by real people. Play, don't watch."** Leans harder on the
   not-big-studios angle. Slightly longer.
2. **"A stable of games. Play, don't watch."** Minimal change from the old description's
   shape; says the least about *why these*.
3. **"Games born from real experience. Play, don't watch."** Closest to Evan's own words;
   "experience" (singular) reads a touch grand.

Rejected:
- Anything with "no ads" / "never pay" — can't promise it, and Evan explicitly doesn't want to.
- Anything about "automation" — the old tagline's word, and the design has moved past it.
- Anything with "browser" — the games are already escaping the browser.
- Anything trying to be funny. (Corny is the enemy.)
