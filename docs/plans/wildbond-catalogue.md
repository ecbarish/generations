# Wildbond's catalogue: meet or beat the originals

**Evan, 2026-10-13:** "I want to meet or beat original Pokémon, so whatever their ratio of forms were, and maybe we even
expand the game to encompass a few areas/versions like Pokémon (which would mean more than the 150 and closer to 2
generations' worth)." Every line follows the evolution rule in [docs/art/wildbond-evolution.md](../art/wildbond-evolution.md):
humble beginnings, spectacular payoff.

## The benchmark

| | Generation 1 (Red/Blue) | Generation 2 (Gold/Silver) | **Wildbond today** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creatures | 151 | 251 (100 new) | about 107 |
| Families | about 79 | about 125 | 89 |
| Three-stage lines | 16 (48 creatures, 32%) | about 25 | 3 (9 creatures, 8%) |
| Two-stage lines | 37 (74 creatures, 49%) | about 50 | 12 (24 creatures, 22%) |
| Branching lines | 1 (Eevee: 4 creatures) | 2 (Eevee 6, plus split lines) | 1 (Poolkit: Rilllynx or Mistlynx) |
| One form | 25 (17%), 5 of them legendary | about 40 | about 74 (70%), 9 of them guardians |
| Regions | 1 | 2 (Johto, then Kanto again) | 1 (the valley, 8 areas and the league) |

**The diagnosis:** we already have as many families as Red/Blue; we have far too few stages. Most of our creatures are
the start of a story with no ending.

## The targets

### Region 1: the valley (Wildbond 2.0 and after)

**151 creatures, matching Red/Blue's shape:**

- **About 16 three-stage lines** (the three starters first, then the strongest promises).
- **About 37 two-stage lines.**
- **Two branching lines** (Poolkit's place-based branch, plus a **bond branch**: one creature that becomes different
  finals depending on how you raised it, our answer to Eevee, and closer to our theme than an item).
- **About 25 one-form creatures, deliberately:** the 9 guardians, the four "never evolves" friends, the three ranch
  hybrids and a handful of rare creatures whose single form is the point.

**How we get there without starting over**, mostly by finishing stories we've already started:

1. **Add stages to existing lines** (starters to three stages first: EVO-1).
2. **Give single-form creatures their payoff:** about 30 of today's singles gain a final form (Drakelet first), and
   some become middle or base forms of others where they already share a family, element and home (lane A proposes
   each pairing; the evolution rule's tests decide).
3. **New creatures only where a region's dex has a gap** (an element missing from an area, a line with no early form).

### Region 2 and beyond: about two generations' worth

Like Gold/Silver: **a second region** of about **100 new creatures**, **new evolutions for region 1 favourites** (a
reason to bring old partners back) and **the first region to revisit** after its league.

- **Where:** the sea beyond Farwatch. The islands you can see from the lookout are already scenery, and "The Crossing"
  (sailing between worlds, on the Incubator list) can become how you get there.
- **Babies:** Gold/Silver added baby species. We already beat that: baby forms are a **life stage for every
  creature** (approved, docs/proposals/creature-growth.md), so every line has a baby, not just eight.
- **About 250 creatures across the two regions**, the same size as Gold/Silver.

### Versions

Pokémon sold two versions with different creatures, so friends had to trade. Our version of that:

- **A choice at the start of the game, not two purchases:** for example a Dawn and a Dusk edition, each with about ten
  creatures you only find in your edition, slightly different story beats, and a different legendary on the box.
- **Trading** to complete the catalogue, through the no-server trade codes already planned (AR3.2), then a shared
  world later.
- Region 2 can repeat the pattern with its own pair.

## When this happens (inside the studio plan)

This is long-term work, and it must not jump ahead of the first impression. It moves through Wildbond's weekly slot:

| Phase (docs/STUDIO-PLAN.md) | Catalogue work |
|---|---|
| **1. First impression** | The starters as three-stage lines, with art that passes the evolution rule (EVO-1, EVO-2). Nothing else from this page |
| **2. Core fun** | Region 1's shape: pairings proposed for every single-form creature, then about 10 new stages a cycle, art and data together, until 151 |
| **3. Launch** | Region 1 complete at launch (or at least the first areas' lines complete, with the rest arriving as "new creatures discovered" updates) |
| **4. Live and grow** | Versions (the edition choice and trading), then Region 2 in seasons: new areas, new creatures, new evolutions for old partners |

**Art is the bottleneck.** 151, then 250, creatures with three-stage silhouettes is a lot of drawing. Before Phase 2:

- decide the art pipeline (ChatGPT's pilot in PR #274 is the starting point);
- confirm the image generator's usage rights (unverified so far, and essential before anything is ever sold);
- pick one style sheet, so a hundred creatures look like one family.
