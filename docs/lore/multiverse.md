# The Generations multiverse (light touch)

Decided 2026-10-08: Evan said yes to a shared universe "where it makes sense". This file is the canon for how the
games connect. Each game's own lore record stays the authority for its world (`docs/lore/realmbound.md`,
`docs/lore/wildbond.md`); this file only adds the threads between them.

## Rules

1. **Every game stands alone.** Nobody needs to play another game to understand or finish one. Crossovers are
   bonuses: a familiar face, a joke, a small reward.
2. **Light touch.** Threads are cameos, shared characters and a few deliberate links, never a shared plot that one
   game has to wait on.
3. **Never contradict a game's own lore.** If a thread would, the game's lore wins and the thread changes.
4. **Recurring characters have one personality everywhere,** but their job fits each world (a peddler in a fantasy
   town, a concession seller at a ballpark).

## How the worlds sit together

- **The Sundered Reach** (Realmbound's continent) is the main fantasy world. **Starfall Guild** is set here too
  (*proposed*): an adventurers' guild in a Reach town, so its heroes, monsters and places can borrow Realmbound's
  names and history. Its own lore takes shape when it's unparked.
- **Wildbond's region** is somewhere else on the same world, across the sea from the Reach (*proposed*), with its
  own peoples and no contact recorded so far. "Faded" and the guardians are local to it.
- **Otherworld** is the bridge: its hero reincarnates life after life, and some lives land in the other games' worlds
  (a farmhand in Thornvale, a tamer's apprentice in Larkhaven, an adventurer in Starfall's guild town). Those lives
  borrow places and people, seen from a newcomer's eyes. Its own home world is still to be designed.
- **Primordial** (back burner) *may* become the deep past of Wildbond's region: the evolution that produced its
  creature families (wolves, boars, sprites…). Only a proposal; it's a hook if Primordial is ever reworked.
- **Diamond Career** (baseball and the sports games) stays in a present-day world. It connects only through recurring
  characters and a joke or two, never through fantasy lore.

## Recurring characters

| Who | Personality | Where they show up |
|---|---|---|
| **Pell**, a travelling peddler with a patient mule called Brisket | Chatty, honest about prices, always "just passing through", claims to have sold a lantern to every hero ever | A shop or cart in each game (Realmbound's roads, Wildbond's towns, Starfall's market, a ballpark snack stand), often with one odd item from another game |
| **The Archivist**, a quiet record-keeper in a grey coat | Curious, kind, never explains where they come from; writes everything down | Hidden spots and late game: a library corner, a ruined tower, a dugout. In Otherworld, the Archivist is the one who records each past life |
| **A mote**, a tiny drifting light | Shy, playful | Wildbond already has Glowmote and Lanternwisp; a matching mote can hide once in each other game as a secret to find |

Pell and the Archivist are new; they can appear the next time a game gets town or story content (no rush). The mote
is optional flavour.

## Crossover rewards (later, through the hub)

`shared/engine.js` already reports each game's progress to the hub. A later, small step: **Arcade titles and
cosmetics** earned in one game and shown in another (finish a Wildbond challenge mode → a creature-shaped trinket
skin in Realmbound; reach a Realmbound raid → a banner for Wildbond's ranch). Never power, only flair, so no game
becomes required homework for another.
