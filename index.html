Play offline or add to your home screen

Open this page online and wait for “Ready for offline play.” You can then open the arcade and its Classic browser games without internet. The new Wildbond and Starfall previews need internet on their first visit. Use Install arcade when it appears, or your browser’s Install / Add to Home Screen menu. Your saves stay in this browser on this device. Without internet, lettering may use device fonts and Wildbond may use its HD-2D view while the 3D renderer is unavailable.

Your saves

Progress is stored in this browser on this device, with automatic backups. Classic browser games have a Your save box to move your journey to another computer. The new previews keep separate saves and do not import Classic journeys.

Automation is earned

Managers, staff and instincts unlock from things you do in the game. Nothing here is for sale.

Always changing

These games get tuned and extended a layer at a time. Prototypes may change a lot between visits.
