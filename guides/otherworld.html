A field guide

Otherworld: three lives

Choose a world, a gift and a name; discover what you carry into the next life.

Checked against the game on 11 November 2026. The three lives below are in the game you can open. This story is still in development, and new work on it comes after Keymaster, the Saltmarsh Shipping Office and Night Market. These pages do not read or change your saves.

Begin in the Between

Follow the narrator into the Between. Choose a world, then read each gift’s own words and its stated cost. Asterhold brings you to Lanthorn, with a beast tide said to be three days out. Hearthmere gives you the shuttered Reedlight Inn, three mornings before the long snow. The Ashen Throne wakes you on a servant’s bed in House Ardel, in the shuttered capital of Veyrin. The house is still standing. The plague is still in the city when the crest later comes down. All three can be played.

Give yourself a name, or pick one of the offered names, and choose a hair colour and a skin colour. The people you meet and the responses you choose shape each life. These are authored portrait scenes and choices, rather than free-moving exploration.

Hearth Cooking can make you late: the pass can tell you the caravan left while you were cooking. Spirit Speech lets you hear Puddle at the inn well. In the kitchen, two neighbors can hear you speaking to empty brick and back away. Green Thumb grows beans and bindweed across the front step, and you can clear that step. If you break the shelter promise, the thread binds your fingers and you cannot carry a bed. You can still tell people where to go, including the river boats. Silence can show you the hidden record of the sacks, and Kael can forget your face. Working beside him and saying your name again is one path. Remaining unseen, and keeping the street lamps, is another.

Read, choose, and take your time

The speech button is labelled Go on. A click or tap on the speech does the same. While letters are still appearing, that first press finishes the line. Hold Go on to walk through the following lines, until a response is waiting. Skip, or Escape, jumps to the end of the current speech. If responses are waiting, they appear. If the scene has no responses, you move on. Number keys 1 to 9 select that numbered response. A closed response stays closed and tells you why. Space and Enter advance a line. When responses are open, Space or Enter selects one only when exactly one response is open. If several are open, they do not choose for you.

Days move when the story moves, not on a clock. Lanthorn’s three days, Reedlight’s mornings and Veyrin’s dawns are scenes, not a countdown. In Asterhold, once the scene at Hesta’s desk begins, Status can open the record of this life. Hearthmere and the Ashen Throne do not use that button.

  • Read what Mira and the other people need, rather than treating each conversation as a reward menu.
  • Available responses can depend on your gift, what happened earlier and remembered knowledge.
  • The browser saves your life as you progress. Returning to the same browser can resume it; clearing browser data can remove it.

An ending is also something you learn

Asterhold has eight endings; Hearthmere has four. The Ashen Throne has five final outcomes and a death route. They are not all victories, and a difficult ending can still teach you something to carry forward. When a life ends, you return to the Between with its record; another life can begin with remembered knowledge. Return is the exception: death rewinds that same Ashen life, keeping learned knowledge while resetting its work and choices. After a returned dawn, you can choose to lay the gift down and leave.

How returning memories work (light spoilers)

Some endings leave memories that affect later scenes and responses. They are recorded knowledge, not a blanket stat upgrade or permission to skip every obstacle. You can choose a new gift and identity for the next life. Memories can travel between worlds: a recipe or something learned by listening may open a new response. Return’s local remembered record survives its dawns but does not become a new soul memory until the life actually ends. Skills that grow through use are a later plan, and they are not in this copy.

A guide should help you understand the choices, not replace them. Try a first life without an ending walkthrough, then decide what you want to do differently.
