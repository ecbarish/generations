# Saltmarsh Shipping Office (IDLE-SS1)

v0.2.1. The office runs until the last voyage. Open it from the hall, under Build and manage.

You row three cargoes yourself, then keep a skiff you can row whenever you like. You buy boats: a Rowboat, a Sloop, a Schooner and a Bark. Capt. Wren takes the pier once enough rowboats are tied up. The sloop shop, the sheds and the harbour log follow a few at a time. At ten sloops, the fleet chart unrolls, and you choose a lighter hull or another shed. The same kind of choice comes back for the schooner holds. Ten schooners open the trade routes. You pick where the next contract goes: a safe run, a risky one, a captain's run, or a fast night run. Twelve barks lengthen the quay and put a choice on the desk, a lamp for safer nights or deeper holds. Fourteen post the tide board. Sixteen light the bar lamp. Five different routes wake the lighthouses. Each light changes the routes, and it shows in the harbour. When the lamps are burning and the chart is full, you send the last voyage. The ending, the credits and the totals stay in the office window. Then you can open a new harbour and ask one captain to come across.

Boats earn the full rate while this page is open, including a hidden tab. Closing the page pays half, and only what the sheds can hold: two hours each, up to four sheds. Both kinds of absence leave a log with the coins you left, the haul totals, and the coins you came back to. Captains are people on a timed haul. They are not a multiplier on the boats. The same stretch of a haul pays once.

The hall card is in the Lab, under Build and manage. Nothing here is taken from another game. `sw.js`, the root `index.html` and `.github/workflows/` are untouched. The tab title reads a steady rate from `shared/idle-core.js`, and the card is `launcher/games.js`.

The save is version 1 (`saltmarsh-shipping-save-v1`). A missing or broken save starts a new office. There is no older save to migrate.

A desk can open a late office from the address bar, for testing. These three addresses work on the live Pages build. They keep their own office in memory and do not write `saltmarsh-shipping-save-v1`:

- `games/saltmarsh-shipping/index.html?state=fleet` opens just after ten schooners, with the trade routes on the desk.
- `games/saltmarsh-shipping/index.html?state=lamps` opens with three lighthouses burning.
- `games/saltmarsh-shipping/index.html?state=final` opens on the last voyage, before it is sent.

Checks: `node tools/run-all-checks.cjs saltmarsh-shipping`

The pacing tool (`node tools/saltmarsh-pacing.cjs`) is a simulation, not a watched play.

## Changelog

- v0.2.1 (2026-11-02, IDLE-SS1b): The first contracts cost about a minute of what the office earns, and a dearer contract asks you to buy a boat instead of waiting. After two routes, the desk offers a pier bell or coal for the night runs. The goal bar is a button: it points at the next thing and scrolls to it. A shed you passed names the base price, the multiplier, and which choice caused it, and it does not say the shed is locked while one can still be raised. After that list is used up, the yard says there is no third shed. A price you cannot pay says how many coins are still needed. The choice sits over the page, so the shop does not jump. Wren and Bramble share one log line each. One coin is one coin on the boat card. A route card shows the next contract. Max stops at the next goal. `?state=fleet`, `?state=lamps` and `?state=final` open late desks without touching a normal save.
- v0.2.0 (2026-11-02, IDLE-SS1b): Trade routes open at ten schooners. Each contract is a real choice of risk, time, pay or a captain's habit. Lighthouses are built along the routes, change how those routes behave, and light up in the harbour. The last voyage ends the office, with credits and a count of the watch, and a new harbour can keep one captain. The hall has a card for the office. The bark prices rise more gently. Minutes 10 to 15 gain the quay choice, the tide board and the bollards. A choice leaves the other side locked on the desk. A shed you pass on costs more, and the count only goes up when you build one. Payback text follows the milestone. The tab title no longer flickers. One coin is one coin. A closed stretch totals the hauls. Hollis has more to say. Wren's count and Bramble's kettle still matter once the routes are sailing.
