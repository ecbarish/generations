# Saltmarsh Shipping Office (IDLE-SS1)

v0.3.0. The office runs until the last voyage. Open it from the Lab, under Build and manage.

You row three cargoes yourself, then keep a skiff you can row whenever you like. You buy boats: a Rowboat, a Sloop, a Schooner and a Bark. Capt. Wren takes the pier once enough rowboats are tied up. The sloop shop, the sheds and the harbour log follow a few at a time. At ten sloops, the fleet chart unrolls, and you choose a lighter hull or another shed. The same kind of choice comes back for the schooner holds. Ten schooners open the trade routes. You pick where the next contract goes: a safe run, a risky one, a captain's run, or a fast night run. Twelve barks lengthen the quay and put a choice on the desk, a lamp for safer nights or deeper holds. Fourteen post the tide board. Sixteen barks light a house lamp in the office window. That lamp is not one of the lighthouses. There are four lighthouses: the Pier Lighthouse, the Headland Lighthouse, the Reed Lighthouse and the Quay Lighthouse. Five different routes wake those four lighthouses. Each lighthouse changes the routes, and it shows in the harbour. A button that cannot be used yet says why, for example while the quay choice is still open. When the four lighthouses are burning, you send the last voyage. A price on the desk stays until you sign that card. A spare sail can be bought for a route that is already sailing. The ending, the credits and the totals stay in the office window. Then you can open a new harbour and ask one captain to come across.

Boats earn the full rate while this page is open, including a hidden tab. Closing the page pays half, and only what the sheds can hold: two hours each, up to four sheds. Both kinds of absence leave a log with the coins you left, the haul totals, and the coins you came back to. Captains are people on a timed haul. They are not a multiplier on the boats. The same stretch of a haul pays once.

The hall card is in the Lab, under Build and manage. Nothing here is taken from another game. `sw.js`, the root `index.html` and `.github/workflows/` are untouched. The tab title reads a steady rate from `shared/idle-core.js`, and the card is `launcher/games.js`.

The save is version 1 (`saltmarsh-shipping-save-v1`). A missing or broken save starts a new office. There is no older save to migrate.

A desk can open a late office from the address bar, for testing. These three addresses work on the live Pages build. They keep their own office in memory and do not write `saltmarsh-shipping-save-v1`:

- `games/saltmarsh-shipping/index.html?state=fleet` opens just after ten schooners, with the trade routes on the desk.
- `games/saltmarsh-shipping/index.html?state=lamps` opens with three lighthouses burning.
- `games/saltmarsh-shipping/index.html?state=final` opens on the last voyage, before it is sent.

Checks: `node tools/run-all-checks.cjs saltmarsh-shipping`

The pacing tool (`node tools/saltmarsh-pacing.cjs`) is a simulation, not a watched play.
