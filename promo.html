Realmbound

A classic-MMO adventure you can play at your desk. Quest, tame, ride and explore dungeons, playing it yourself when you have a minute and letting it run when you don't.

Play Realmbound See the whole arcade Free, in your browser, no account. Works on phones. Your progress saves on your device.

What's in it today

Two factions, five classes, five zones across the world and two dungeons, levels 1 to 40.

Play it, or let it play

Flip between Focus and Auto any time. Pressing your abilities yourself always pays better: you catch openings, dodge boss attacks and earn bonus XP. Auto keeps you leveling while you work.

Automation you earn

Helpers unlock through play. Loot by hand 60 times to earn AutoLoot. Use your abilities to earn the Rotation Macro. Each addon takes a chore off your hands because you've done it yourself.

Tame wild beasts

Hunters tame beasts their level or lower, then fight together until they're bonded. Wild beasts come in rarities:

CommonUncommonRareEpicLegendary

Mounts that get better

Save up for your first horse or wolf, or teach your bonded pet to carry you. Every mount trains as you ride it, from Green all the way to Champion.

People who remember you

Named adventurers wander the world beside you, each with their own personality. Help them, group up, share loot. Friends unlock combined attacks mid-fight.

Dungeons that never end

Gather five, learn the bosses, dodge the big hits. Every clear unlocks a harder Heroic tier with random twists and better loot. There is no top.

The Winter Road

Continue into Frostmere for levels 30–40. Take ten quests from the people of Lanternrest Lodge or Whitebough Hearth, face ice trolls, and seek Hushfang, a legendary wolf elite.

A world with a voice

Each zone has its own lore, and quest givers answer in their own words when you turn in their quests. Meet the people behind the work, from your faction's first outpost to the snowbound highlands.
